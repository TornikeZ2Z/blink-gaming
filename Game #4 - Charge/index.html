Your Brainer · brain level 1

Your Brainer grows with every round you play, in every Brainer game: each round adds the same brain cells when it ends, whatever you bet. 100 levels, a new look every 10. Brain levels change how your Brainer looks, never the odds. In Charge, every round still starts from the baby on the pad.

Provably fair

Every round is fixed before you bet, by the game's server seed and your client seed. You see the SHA-256 hash of the server seed first, then set your client seed if you like; the server seed is revealed when the round ends. Each charge rolls HMAC-SHA256(server seed, "client seed:nonce:charge"), read as a fraction from its first 52 bits, and a roll at or above that charge's safe chance overcharges the Brainer.

How to play
  1. Pick a difficulty and your bet, then press Play. Your Brainer starts on the charging pad at Level 1, 1.00×.
  2. Press Charge. A safe charge levels it up and raises your multiplier. As the multiplier grows, so does your Brainer: new looks from 1.2×, up to the master with the graduation cap and cape at 1,000×.
  3. Cash out whenever you like to take your win, up to $10,000 a round. If a charge overloads, your Brainer breaks down and the bet is lost.
  4. Harder difficulties hide more overloads (1, 3, 5 or 10 in 25 charges), so each charge pays more.
  5. Your Brainer grows with every round: each one adds brain cells, whatever you bet. Brain levels never change the odds.

RTP up to 98%. The return is 98% before two things lower it in some rounds: payouts round down to the cent, and the $10,000 max win ends long or high-bet rounds early.
