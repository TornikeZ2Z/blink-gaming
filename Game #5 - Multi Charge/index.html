Provably fair

Every round is fixed before you bet, by the game's server seed and your client seed. You see the SHA-256 hash of the server seed first, then set your client seed if you like; the server seed is revealed when the round ends. Each charge is one yes or no: HMAC-SHA256(server seed, "client seed:nonce:charge"), read as a fraction from its first 52 bits, is a no at or above that charge's safe chance. The pillar you pick only shows that answer and can't change it. The other pillars are for show: one to three of them look overloaded at random and the rest safe, the same on a win and a loss. A roll of "client seed:nonce:charge:show:0" says how many, and rolls of "client seed:nonce:charge:show:pillar" say which.

RTP up to 97%. The return is 97% before two things lower it in some rounds: payouts round down to the cent, and the $10,000 max win ends long or high-bet rounds early.

How to play
  1. Pick a difficulty and your bet, then press Play.
  2. Pick one of five pillars. A safe one levels your Brainer up and raises your multiplier.
  3. Cash out to take your win. An overloaded pillar loses the bet.
  4. Harder difficulties overload more, so each charge pays more.
