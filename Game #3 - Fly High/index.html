Your Brainer · level 1

Your Brainer grows with every round you play: each one adds the same brain cells when it ends, whatever you bet. 100 levels, a new look every 10. Levels change how your Brainer looks, never the odds.

Provably fair

Every round is fixed before take-off, by the game's server seed and your client seed. You see the SHA-256 hash of the server seed first, then set your client seed if you like; the seed is revealed when the round ends. The round rolls HMAC-SHA256(server seed, "client seed:nonce"), read as a fraction u from its first 52 bits, and flies away at 0.98 ÷ (1 − u), rounded down to the cent (1.00× at least, 10,000× at most).

How to play
  1. Place a bet before take-off. You can place two bets at once, one in each panel.
  2. Your Brainer takes off and the multiplier climbs.
  3. Cash out before it flies away to win your bet times the multiplier, up to $10,000 a round. If it flies away first, the bet is lost.
  4. Auto places your bet every round and can cash out by itself at a multiplier you choose.
  5. Your Brainer grows with every round you play: each one adds brain cells, whatever you bet. Levels never change the odds.

RTP up to 98%. The return is 98% before two things lower it in some rounds: payouts round down to the cent, and the $10,000 max win ends high-bet rounds early.
