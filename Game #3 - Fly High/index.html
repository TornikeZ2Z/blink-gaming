Provably fair

Every round is fixed before take-off, by the game's server seed and your client seed. You see the SHA-256 hash of the server seed first, then set your client seed if you like; the seed is revealed when the round ends. The round rolls HMAC-SHA256(server seed, "client seed:nonce"), read as a fraction u from its first 52 bits, and flies away at 0.97 ÷ (1 − u), rounded down to the cent (1.00× at least, 10,000× at most). Scares like a sputtering boot are only for show and are not part of the round.

How to play
  1. Place a bet before take-off. You can place two bets at once, one in each panel.
  2. The Brainer fires its jets and the multiplier climbs. The higher it goes, the more gear it bolts on, different every round, up to suit level 10.
  3. Cash out before it flies away to win your bet times the multiplier, up to $10,000 a round. If it flies away first, the bet is lost.
  4. Auto places your bet every round and can cash out by itself at a multiplier you choose.
  5. A sputtering boot or a glitching screen is only a scare. Scares come at random and never tell you when it will fly away.

RTP up to 97%. The return is 97% before two things lower it in some rounds: payouts round down to the cent, and the $10,000 max win ends high-bet rounds early.
