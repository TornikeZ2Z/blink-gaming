Provably fair

Every round, fixed before the bet

Nobody can change how a round ends once it starts, where the bombs are or where the Brainer flies away, and anyone can prove it afterwards: the player, the casino or a regulator.

How it works

Commit, play, verify

  1. Commit

    Before the round, the game makes a secret server seed and shows its SHA-256 hash in its fairness sheet (the shield button). The hash fixes the seed without giving it away.

  2. Play

    Then the player can set their own client seed in the same sheet, or keep the random one. Every roll in every game comes from the server seed, the client seed and the round number. Which way the player goes, or when they cash out, cannot change it.

  3. Verify

    When the round ends the server seed is revealed and the next one is committed. Hash it again and redraw the round: if both match, the round was fixed before the bet.

The scheme

Exactly what the games do

In these demos the game in your browser plays the server’s part, so every step can be inspected.

Server seed
32 random bytes, written as 64 lowercase hex characters. The game makes each round’s seed, and shows the SHA-256 of those 64 characters as its hash, before the player can choose the client seed for that round. Every round gets a new seed, and the old one is revealed when its round ends.
Client seed
Starts as 16 random hex characters. In the fairness sheet the player can type 1 to 64 characters under “Your client seed”, counted as Unicode code points (most letters and emoji count as one; a flag or a joined emoji such as a family counts as several), with any spaces or other whitespace at either end dropped, or press “New random”. A change counts from the next round, and the field is locked while a round is on. It is kept for the session only.
Nonce
The round number: 1 for the first round after the game loads, then one more for every round.
Roll
HMAC-SHA256(server_seed, message), with the seed’s 64 hex characters as the key and both key and message as UTF-8 text. The first 13 hex digits of the result (52 bits), divided by 252, give a roll from 0 up to 1.
Plane Crash
One roll for every path at every step, with the message client_seed:nonce:step:path: steps count from 1, and paths go 1 to 5 as numbered on the hoops. A path holds a bomb when its roll is at or above that step’s survival chance, (26 − step − bombs) ÷ (26 − step), with 1, 3, 5 or 10 bombs by difficulty.
Space Crash
Plane Crash’s rolls, with drones for paths: one roll for every drone in every row, with the message client_seed:nonce:row:drone, rows counting from 1 and drones 1 to 5 from left to right as numbered in the game. A drone’s shield holds when its roll is at or above that row’s survival chance, the same as Plane Crash’s, with 1, 3, 5 or 10 holding shields by difficulty. The shields’ tougher looks deeper in a round never change the rolls.
Multi Charge
Plane Crash’s rolls, with pillars for paths: one roll for every pillar at every charge, with the message client_seed:nonce:charge:pillar, charges counting from 1 and pillars 1 to 5 from left to right as numbered in the game. A pillar is overloaded when its roll is at or above that charge’s survival chance, the same as Plane Crash’s, with 1, 3, 5 or 10 overloads by difficulty.
Charge
One roll for every charge, with the message client_seed:nonce:charge, charges counting from 1. A charge overloads and ends the round when its roll is at or above that charge’s survival chance, the same as a Plane Crash step’s: (26 − charge − n) ÷ (26 − charge), where n is 1, 3, 5 or 10 by difficulty.
Fly High
One roll for every round, with the message client_seed:nonce. The Brainer flies away at 0.98 ÷ (1 − roll), rounded down to the cent, never below 1.00× (about 3 rounds in 100 fly away at take-off) and never above 10,000×. Rounds run on their own, so the nonce counts every round, with or without a bet; the client seed can change while bets are open, and the one in effect at take-off is that round’s. Each of the two bets pays at most $10,000 and cashes out when it gets there, while the round flies on. The demo also has a Test flight field under the bets that sets the fly-away point by hand, for testing; rounds flown that way are marked as test rounds in the history and the fairness sheet, which also shows the point the seeds gave.
Payouts
Bet × multiplier, with the multiplier and the payout each rounded down to the cent. A round pays at most the $10,000 max win: one that reaches it ends there and pays $10,000.
