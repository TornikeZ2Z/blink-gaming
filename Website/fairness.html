Provably fair

Every round, fixed before the first bet

Nobody can change a result once a round starts, and anyone can prove it afterwards: the player, the casino or a regulator.

How it works

Commit, play, verify

  1. Commit

    Before the round, the server picks a secret seed and publishes its SHA-256 hash. The hash fixes the round without giving it away.

  2. Play

    Where the bombs fall at every step comes from the seed alone, so no choice made during the round can change it.

  3. Verify

    After the round the seed is revealed. Hash it again: if it matches the published hash, the round was fixed in advance.
