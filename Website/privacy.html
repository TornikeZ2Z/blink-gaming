Privacy

No accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no trackers. Here is exactly what this site keeps, and where.

What stays in your browser

The site and the demos remember a few settings in your browser’s local storage. They never leave your device:

  • blink.age-ok: that you confirmed you are 18 or older, so we do not ask again.
  • brainer.pc.tutorial and brainer.fr.tutorial: that you have seen how to play Plane Crash and FogRush.
  • brainer.sound: whether the game sound is on.
  • brainer.xp and brainer.level: your Brainer’s brain cells and brain level, shared by the demos.
  • brainer.world: which world you last flew in, in Plane Crash.
  • brainer.fr.auto: the auto cash-out you picked in FogRush.
  • brainer.intro (kept only until you close the tab): that the Brainer Games intro has already played.

You can delete them at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings. The demo balance is not stored.

What we do not do

We use no cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party scripts or fonts. Every file on this site, fonts included, comes from this site.

The contact form

The contact form is not connected yet, so nothing you type in it is sent or stored. We will update this page before it goes live.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors’ IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.
