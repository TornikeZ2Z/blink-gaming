Responsible gaming

Play for fun, never for money you need

Our games are for adults aged 18 and over, in markets where online gambling is legal.

The demos use play money

The demos on this site use play money in US dollars. It has no value, cannot be bought, won or withdrawn, and the balance resets whenever you like.

How the odds work

Every round is random and fixed before you bet. Your choices never change the odds: in Plane Crash every path at a step has the same chance of a bomb, and in FogRush every new tile has the same 8% chance whichever way you walk. No pattern or system can beat it. The RTP is a long-run average over a very large number of rounds. In a single session you can lose everything you bet.

Your Brainer’s brain level only changes how it looks and which worlds you can fly in. It never changes the RTP, the chance of a bomb or any payout, and every finished round earns the same brain cells whatever you bet.

When you play for real money

  • Set a budget and a time limit before you start, and stop when you reach either.
  • Only play with money you can afford to lose. Never borrow to gamble.
  • Do not chase losses. A loss is the cost of the entertainment, not a debt to win back.
  • Take breaks, and do not play when you are upset, tired or drinking.
  • Use the tools your casino must offer: deposit limits, time-outs, reality checks and self-exclusion.

Where to find help

If gambling stops being fun, or you are worried about someone else, talk to someone. These services are free and confidential:

  • Gambling Therapy: online support in many languages, worldwide.
  • Gamblers Anonymous: meetings and contacts in many countries.
  • GamCare: advice and a helpline in the UK.
