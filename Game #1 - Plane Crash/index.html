Provably fair

Every flight is fixed before you bet, by the game's server seed and your client seed. You see the SHA-256 hash of the server seed first, then set your client seed if you like; the server seed is revealed when the flight ends. Each path at each step rolls HMAC-SHA256(server seed, "client seed:nonce:step:path"), and a roll at or above that step's survival chance is a bomb.

How to play
  1. Pick a difficulty and your bet, then press Play.
  2. Bombs fall ahead. Choose one of five paths. Every safe path raises your multiplier.
  3. Jump with the parachute whenever you like to take your win, up to $10,000 a flight. A bomb on your path ends the flight.

RTP up to 97%. The return is 97% before two things lower it in some flights: payouts round down to the cent, and the $10,000 max win ends long or high-bet flights early.
