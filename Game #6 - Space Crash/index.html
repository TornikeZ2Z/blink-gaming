Provably fair

Every round is fixed before you bet, by the game's server seed and your client seed. You see the SHA-256 hash of the server seed first, then set your client seed if you like; the server seed is revealed when the round ends. Drone k of row n rolls HMAC-SHA256(server seed, "client seed:nonce:n:k"), and a roll at or above that row's survival chance is a shield that holds.

How to play
  1. Pick a difficulty and your bet, then press Play.
  2. Five shield drones block each row. Pick one and fire. Break its shield and you fly on, and your multiplier rises.
  3. Cash out whenever you like to take your win, up to $10,000 a round. If the shield you shoot holds, your pod powers down and the round ends.

RTP up to 98%. The return is 98% before two things lower it in some flights: payouts round down to the cent, and the $10,000 max win ends long or high-bet flights early.
