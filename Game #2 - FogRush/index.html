Your Brainer · level 1

The same Brainer as in Plane Crash: it grows in every Brainer game. Each finished round adds the same brain cells, whatever you bet. 100 levels, a new look every 10. Levels change how your Brainer looks, never the odds.

Provably fair

Every map is fixed before you bet. You see the SHA-256 hash of the server seed first; the seed itself is revealed when the round ends. Each tile rolls HMAC-SHA256(server seed, "client seed:nonce:column:row"): a roll under 8% is a bomb, and the rest decides plain tile or chest. The start tile is always safe.

How to play
  1. Pick your bet and press Play. Your Brainer lands in a foggy valley, next to the airfield Plane Crash flies from.
  2. Walk with the arrows, WASD, or by tapping a tile next to your Brainer. Every new tile clears the clouds and raises your multiplier; some hide chests from ×1.3 to ×25.
  3. 8% of new tiles hide a bomb, which ends the round. Tiles you've already cleared are always safe to cross.
  4. Cash out whenever you like. Your Brainer grows with every round, whatever you bet.
